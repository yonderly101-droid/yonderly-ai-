Connect your WhatsApp (5 steps, one-time)

  1. Complete your business profile in Yonderly first — the AI uses it to answer customers.
  2. Add your business number in Meta. Open Meta Business Settings → WhatsApp accounts → add the number customers already use. Verify it by SMS.
  3. Move the number to the API. The line cannot stay active in the normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business phone app at the same time — Meta will switch it to automated messaging.
  4. Copy your Phone number ID. In Meta go to WhatsApp Manager, select your number, then open API / developer settings. Copy the long Phone number ID (numbers only). If you cannot find it, contact Yonderly support — we can help on a quick call.
  5. Paste below and click Link. Your customers keep messaging the same number on your website — they never see these IDs.
Troubleshooting
  • Do not use Meta’s +1 555 test number for real customers.
  • If linking fails, try adding the WABA ID from the same Meta page.
  • Make sure your Yonderly business profile is saved before linking.
